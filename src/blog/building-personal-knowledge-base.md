A personal knowledge base (PKB) is essential for anyone who learns continuously. Here's my approach to building and maintaining one.

## Why Obsidian?

Obsidian provides a local-first, markdown-based approach to note-taking that aligns perfectly with how I think. The linking system allows for organic knowledge connections.

## My Setup

1. **Daily Notes** - Quick capture for thoughts and ideas
2. **Literature Notes** - Summaries from books and articles
3. **Permanent Notes** - Refined concepts and insights
4. **Structure Notes** - Overview pages linking related topics

## Tips

- Keep it simple initially
- Review and refine regularly
- Link liberally between notes
- Use templates for consistency

Building a PKB is a long-term investment that compounds over time. Start small and let it grow organically.
